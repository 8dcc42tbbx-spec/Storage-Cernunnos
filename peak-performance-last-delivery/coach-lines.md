# Coach's Line Pool

The full set of caption lines for Coach's radio popups (see `README.md`
§5 for the character, her trigger points, and the "captions, not sampled
voice" delivery mechanism this pool feeds).

**Tone rule for every line in this file:** fitness/sports-coaching
language run through Australia Post's Christmas "Peak" season and parcel
delivery — the same pun the game's title makes. Keep lines short enough
to read inside the couple of seconds her portrait is on screen, and keep
them landing as *tone* (upbeat, sporty, a little cheesy) even for a
player who doesn't catch the specific wordplay.

**Rotation rule:** for any pool with more than one line, pick at random
each time the trigger fires, excluding whichever line played last for
that same trigger (a simple "don't immediately repeat" rule) — that's
enough variety for a pool this size across several replays without
needing a bigger bank. Pool sizes below were picked with that in mind:
big for triggers that fire constantly (overtakes), small for triggers
that fire once a run (leg starts, run end).

---

2. "Nice split! Keep that cadence."
3. "That's a PB overtake, driver — mark it down."
4. "Smooth as a gift-wrap corner. Keep flowing."
5. "Drafted right past 'em — save those legs for the sprint."
6. "That's the form, right there. Textbook."
7. "One more parcel closer, one more rep done."
8. "Tidy pass! Ref's giving that a ten."
9. "Feel that? That's Peak season fitness."
10. "Straight through, no fouls. Keep the tempo up."

### Checkpoint / gate clear — a bigger deal than a plain overtake

1. "Checkpoint's ticked off — that's a full set complete!"
2. "Gate's clear! Bank that time, driver, you earned it."
3. "That's a personal best split — the clock just said thank you."
4. "Milestone down! Hydrate — well, keep driving, but you know."
5. "Photo finish energy at that gate. Loved it."
6. "That's your second wind kicking in right on schedule."
7. "Gate cleared with time in the bank — that's championship form."
8. "Tick that box — literally, there's a parcel in it."

### Low-clock warning — at most once per leg, so keep it urgent

1. "Clock doesn't do extra time tonight, driver — dig in."
2. "This is the final kilometre. Leave it all on the road."
3. "No cool-down laps left — full send to the line."
4. "Heart rate up, driver — that's exactly where it should be."
5. "Second wind time. Find it. Use it."
6. "The depot's not doing penalties tonight — make the clock, don't chase it."

## Leg 1 — Suburban Sprint (afternoon, clear)

**Leg start**
1. "Warm-up lap, driver — easy pace, eyes open, plenty of parked cars playing defence."
2. "First leg of the circuit — suburbs are your stretching routine before the real work."

**Hazard: parked car pulling out**
1. "Watch the driveways — that's an unmarked opponent stepping onto the track."
2. "Parked car, pulling out — treat it like a surprise substitution."

**Hazard: kid on a bike**
1. "Junior league on the footpath — give 'em room, champ."
2. "Bike on the road — that's someone's training run too. Be a good coach."

**Hazard: wheelie-bin day**
1. "Bin day! Dodgeball round, driver — don't clip the green ones."

**Leg clear**

**Leg start**

**Hazard: road train**
1. "Road train ahead — that's not an opponent, that's a whole relay team. Patient overtake."
2. "Big rig, long pass — treat it like a slow lap, not a sprint finish."

**Hazard: spray / reduced visibility behind trucks**
1. "Spray's up behind that truck — like fogged-up goggles. Back off and reset."

**Leg clear**
1. "Highway's behind you — that's the marathon leg done. Backroads are shorter, but sneakier."

## Leg 4 — Mountain Pass (deep night, snow/ice)

**Leg start**
1. "Climbing leg, driver — this is your hill-sprint set. Lungs and tyres both working overtime."
2. "Altitude training, unofficially. Keep the pace controlled on the way up."

**Self-aware snow wink** (fires once, near leg start or first snowfall)
1. "Yeah, I know it doesn't usually snow here either — call it a bonus interval. Just get up the hill."
2. "Don't ask me why there's snow on an Australian Christmas Eve. Coach doesn't do weather reports, just hill reps."

**Hazard: black ice**
1. "Ice patch! That's a balance drill nobody signed up for — ease off, don't fight it."
2. "Slippery footing ahead. Soft hands on the wheel, like it's a hurdle you don't want to clip."

**Hazard: snowdrift debris**
1. "Drift across the road — like someone left the obstacle course out overnight. Steer smart."
2. "Snow's piling up out there. Treat it like wet sand — commit, don't hesitate."

**Leg clear**
1. "Summit's done! That's your hardest set of the night behind you. One leg left — the big one."
2. "Down off the mountain and still upright — that's a coaching win right there. Final stretch, driver."

**Leg start**
1. "This is it — the finals. Everything you've trained for tonight comes down to this leg."

**Hazard: whiteout gust**
1. "Can't see the line? Trust your legs — well, your tyres. You know this road by feel now."

1. "Porch light's close now, driver. This is your finishing kick — everything you've got, right here."
2. "One parcel, one house, one shot at gold. Bring it home."

## Run end

### Success

1. "Gold-medal delivery! That's how you finish a Peak season — flying colours, right under the tree."
2. "Delivered! That's a personal best in every category coach tracks. Go put your feet up — you've earned it."
3. "Photo finish, parcel in hand. That's champion form, driver. Merry Christmas."
4. "Full marks. Clean route, clock in hand, parcel delivered. Best session of the Peak, no contest."
5. "That's the whistle — full time, and you won it. Go enjoy Christmas, you've more than earned it."

### Failure

1. "Didn't stick the landing tonight, but that's a Peak-season effort right there. Go again?"
2. "Clock beat us this time. Happens to everyone once — even coach's had a bad session. Reset and go again."
3. "Not the finish we wanted, but you left it all on the road. That counts for plenty. Again?"
4. "Tough one. But you know what — most people don't even lace up for a night like this. Proud of you regardless."
5. "That's a DNF, not a failure, driver. Big difference. Get some rest — we go again next Peak."

## Implementation note

This maps directly onto the `coach.js` module noted in `README.md` §9: a
plain data array per trigger/pool (mirroring how the sibling game in this
repo keeps its own large content bank, `trivia-trixie/js/questions.js`,
separate from game logic), a "don't repeat the last line for this trigger"
pick function, and the queue described in §5/§6 so nothing overlaps the
milestone banner. Extending any pool later is just appending strings —
no code changes needed.
